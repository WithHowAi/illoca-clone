An Open Letter

To those who shape the world,

Design was never meant to happen through buttons, commands and rigid workflows. It begins more naturally — through sketches, markups, references, conversation and iteration, in the back-and-forth between instinct and judgment.

For too long that flow has been interrupted, asking you to translate, redraw, rebuild and re-explain instead of simply designing.

We built Tracing Paper for the way this work actually happens: a place where intent can be expressed directly, and the production burden fades into the background — so you can explore more freely, refine more quickly, and stay closer to the ideas that matter most.

This is for you.

Generic AI produces static images; Tracing Paper produces structured, editable 2D and 3D design data driven by sketches and markups, and it plugs into the CAD/BIM tools studios already use instead of replacing them.

Two modes: Ideate — a sketch or prompt becomes drawings; and Iterate — redlines and markups update geometry and documentation in real time. Work exports into tools such as Autodesk Revit.

The workflow is human-in-the-loop: you direct with sketches and plain language — no prompt engineering — and your professional judgment stays in control of the result.

You own your designs. Your sketches, prompts and models are not used to train the system; enterprise plans add stricter retention controls.

Credits are charged for generated output (plans, massing, exports), not for navigating or sketching. Plans include a monthly allowance, and new sign-ups start with 1,000 starter credits.
